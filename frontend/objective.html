Objectives & Functions

Primary Objectives

Watt-Wise is designed with the objective of optimizing renewable energy usage at the local level by enabling transparent and efficient peer-to-peer solar energy trading. The platform aims to reduce energy wastage, improve grid stability, and promote clean energy adoption.

  • Maximize utilization of locally generated solar energy
  • Enable transparent energy transactions between buyers and sellers
  • Reduce dependency on centralized power grids
  • Support smart-city and sustainability initiatives
  • Encourage responsible energy consumption

Core Functions

Watt-Wise performs real-time monitoring of energy generation, consumption, and storage using smart meters and intelligent analytics. The system dynamically adjusts energy pricing based on availability, demand, and time of day.

  • Real-time energy monitoring and analytics
  • AI-based demand and supply forecasting
  • Secure digital payment integration
  • Automated billing and usage reports
  • Role-based dashboards for owners and buyers

By integrating advanced technology with renewable infrastructure, Watt-Wise acts as a bridge between energy producers and consumers, ensuring efficiency, transparency, and long-term sustainability.
