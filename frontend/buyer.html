Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is Watt-Wise and how does it work?

Watt-Wise is a hyper-local solar energy trading platform that enables users to buy and sell excess solar power within a community using real-time data, smart analytics, and secure digital payments.

2. How is the price of electricity calculated?

Electricity is priced per kilowatt-hour (kWh) based on a base rate (₹8 per unit) while considering availability, demand, and system conditions. This ensures fair and transparent pricing.

3. Can I purchase electricity at night?

Yes. During night hours, energy is supplied using stored solar power and grid-balanced electricity to ensure uninterrupted service.

4. How does Watt-Wise help reduce energy wastage?

Excess solar energy that would otherwise be unused is redistributed locally to nearby consumers, reducing transmission losses and wastage.

5. Is the payment process secure?

All payments are processed through Razorpay, which follows bank-grade encryption standards and secure transaction protocols.

6. Can users track their energy usage and payments?

Yes. Watt-Wise provides real-time dashboards that show energy consumption, total units purchased, transaction history, and payment summaries.

7. Who can sell electricity on Watt-Wise?

Registered users with rooftop solar installations or renewable energy sources can sell surplus electricity to nearby buyers on the platform.

8. How does artificial intelligence improve the system?

AI models are used to forecast energy generation and demand, optimize pricing, and balance loads to maintain grid stability.

9. How does Watt-Wise support smart city initiatives?

The platform promotes decentralized energy distribution, reduces carbon emissions, supports EV charging, and aligns with smart-city sustainability goals.

10. What future features are planned?

Future enhancements include battery storage integration, mobile applications, blockchain-based billing, and carbon credit tracking.
