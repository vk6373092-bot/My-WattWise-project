Vinit Kumar

Project Lead & Full Stack Developer – Watt-Wise

Vinit Kumar is the primary architect and developer of the Watt-Wise platform. With a strong interest in renewable energy systems and smart-city technologies, he has led the design and implementation of the project from concept to execution.

His role includes frontend and backend development, system integration, UI/UX design, database management, and real-time analytics implementation. He has also worked on payment integration and live data simulation for energy monitoring.

Through Watt-Wise, Vinit aims to contribute toward sustainable development goals by leveraging technology to promote clean energy adoption and decentralized power management.
